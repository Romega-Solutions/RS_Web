## Core Service Offerings

**RPO — Recruitment Process Outsourcing**
End-to-end, on-demand hiring at scale. Romega's dedicated RPO program maps the global talent market, applies a culture-fit diagnostic, and delivers an evaluated shortlist within one week. Critical positions are filled in roughly two weeks, with a 12-month retention rate above 95%. Designed for companies needing to move fast without sacrificing quality.

**BPO — Business Process Outsourcing**
Scalable support for tech-driven teams. Flexible BPO teams operate around the clock on client systems, following ISO quality controls to manage customer support and back-office work. Reduces operational expenses by up to 30% while improving customer satisfaction — freeing internal staff to focus on core business functions.

**Strategic HR — People Strategy & Advisory**
Senior HR advisors align people strategy with business goals, covering org design, headcount planning, and culture programs. Engagements begin with a review of current practice, followed by an action plan and guided rollout. Measurable results include lower turnover and stronger engagement within one quarter.

**Quality Hire — Executive Search**
Focused searches for high-grade leaders in finance, operations, sales, and product. Uses data analytics and structured interviews to identify candidates who fit both culture and goals. Positions typically fill in approximately four weeks, with new leaders reaching full productivity faster and earning higher stakeholder trust than traditional search methods.

**Mentoring — Leadership Coaching**
Structured one-to-one coaching for newly promoted managers, delivered by seasoned technology executives. Monthly sessions, shared playbooks, and progress tracking help new leaders hit targets twice as fast and reduce team turnover within the first quarter.

**Teaching — Workforce Upskilling**
Instructor-led AI and machine-learning workshops built around the client's live codebase and data. Includes 30 days of follow-up support post-workshop. Teams ship AI features up to four weeks faster, and staff earn certificates that demonstrate new capability to stakeholders.

## Differentiating Features

**Culture Fit Diagnostic**
A proprietary four-step process (Discover → Map → Validate → Report) that converts company values into measurable hiring criteria. Every shortlisted candidate is rated against a culture scorecard alongside technical criteria. Clients receive a side-by-side report with culture-fit scores, key strengths, risks, and a clear hire/no-hire recommendation with onboarding guidance.

**Global Talent Network**
Access to executive and specialist talent across technology, finance, healthcare, manufacturing, and professional services — supporting remote, hybrid, and on-site hiring across industries worldwide.

**Confidential Talent Pool**
A curated pool of pre-vetted professionals, fully confidential and GDPR-compliant. Candidates' journeys stay private; roles stay protected. Trusted by industry leaders with a 4.9/5 aggregate rating across 127 reviews.

**Career Page & Employer Branding**
Branded career site presence supporting client employer branding, integrated with the talent acquisition workflow to attract culture-aligned candidates from the first touchpoint.

**Flexible Engagement Models**
Pricing designed for different stages of growth: fixed-fee retainers, per-hire fees, and monthly subscriptions. Engagements range from short-term project support (3 months) to multi-year strategic partnerships.

**Post-Hire Support**
90-day check-ins and structured onboarding support after placement to protect the investment and ensure new leaders integrate successfully.
